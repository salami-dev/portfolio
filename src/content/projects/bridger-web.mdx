## Financing lifecycle

The backend represented the path from financing origination and identity checks through wallet activity, payments, and repayment. Each stage changed what actions were valid and what operations needed to happen next.

## Failure boundaries

Identity verification, money movement, and repayment can fail independently. The domain boundaries needed to keep those states distinguishable so an interrupted step did not make the whole financing lifecycle ambiguous.
