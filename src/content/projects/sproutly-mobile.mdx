## Mobile support

My contribution sat behind the mobile client: backend capabilities, deployment operations, and the infrastructure supporting the application.

## Service reliability

Failures in those supporting layers surfaced to users through the app even when the client itself behaved correctly. Backend and deployment changes therefore had to preserve the service behavior the mobile experience relied on.
