## System boundary

The mobile product depended on backend services that also received device-originated health data. The platform needed a clear boundary between mobile-client behavior, wearable-data ingestion, backend processing, and production operations.

## Wearable-data integration

Wearable sources do not necessarily describe health records in the same shape or deliver them on the same schedule. The backend redesign had to accommodate those integration differences while preserving data the product could use and the team could operate in production.
