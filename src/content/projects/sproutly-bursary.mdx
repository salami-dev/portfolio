## Tuition-payment workflow

The product supported tuition payments from initiation through backend processing and operational handling. My scope covered architecture, payment behavior, backend contributions, and the delivery environment.

## Recovery state

A payment could be started without every downstream step completing successfully. The workflow needed explicit state so users and operators could distinguish progress, failure, and the actions available for recovery.
